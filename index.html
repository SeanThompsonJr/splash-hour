Design concept, not a real event.

Splash Hour is made up. It’s a design piece made for an Instagram post, to show what a Minecraft event website could look like if it were built entirely from blocks.

Anything with a Sample sign is invented, including the event name, time, server address, plan, rules and contact. The countdown really ticks, but there’s no event to join, and the email form doesn’t send anything.

Splash Hour, a free live Minecraft event. Meet at the waterfall.

Splash Hour Sample is a free, live meetup inside Minecraft. Once a week everyone logs in at the same time, gathers at the falls, and spends an hour building, racing and hanging out.

How to join

Any skin, any skill level. All you need is Minecraft and an internet connection.

  1. Open Minecraft: Java Edition

    Use version 1.21 or newer. Sample

  2. Add the server

    Choose Multiplayer, then Add Server, and paste this address: Sample

    play.splashhour.example

  3. Join at start time

    You’ll spawn beside the waterfall. Hosts wear gold helmets Sample, so you can spot them if you need a hand.

What happens in the hour Sample

Every activity is short and works for first-timers. Join in, or just watch from the pool.

  1. 0:00

    Gather at the falls

    Say hi in chat, find a spot by the pool and see who’s here.

  2. 0:10

    Build round

    Everyone gets the same small plot and 20 minutes to build to a theme.

  3. 0:35

    Race to the top

    A parkour course up the cliff beside the waterfall. Go at your own pace.

  4. 0:55

    Wave goodbye

    Head back to the falls, show off your build and hear next week’s theme.

House rules Sample

  • Be kind in chat. Everyone’s welcome, whatever their age or skill.
  • Build only on your own plot unless someone invites you in.
  • No griefing, stealing or trapping other players.
  • Need help? Find a host in a gold helmet, or ask in chat.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Splash Hour is free to join and free to watch.

Do I need to be good at Minecraft?

Not at all. Every activity works for first-timers, and you can sit any of them out.

What if I can’t make it this week?

It happens every week at the same time, so you can catch the next one. Add it to your calendar or sign up for a reminder.
